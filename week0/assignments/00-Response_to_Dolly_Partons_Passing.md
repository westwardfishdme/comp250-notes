# Paper on a Single Quote
**Date:** 08-26-2026
## My quote:
> "It is but human nature to take its most advanced creation and turn it into a machination for war, death, and money."

This isn't a quotation from somebody else, it's a quote by me. I wrote it one night upon reflecting upon my future as
a cybersecurity student; as a programmer; and as someone observing the world. While it is not my fundamental belief
that humanity is necessarily violent, or starved for war; but rather there exists an invisible force that drives a group
of people to maintain their vision of order. Some people call this power, some people call it money, but almost one thing
is certain: so long as there is a way to maintain power-- those who own this power will give all in their might to maintain
it. 

This remains true regardless of ideology, and politics; but do not confuse my words for saying that greed and hyperindividualism
are a fundamental part of human nature; On the contrary-- I believe that there is no one way to define humanity with the exception
that people are the embodiment of a partially predictable randomness; of which can be determined by through the study of economics
and the study of class. 

The thing about technology is that it encompasses a whole variety of different areas that we don't normally think about, including firearms. 
We don't often think of computer as a gun but they nonetheless are on the same caliber, if not more dangerous. We can use technology to build 
things as powerful as the atom bomb; but the same power of the atom can be used to produce vast amounts of electricity that would be the equivalent 
to 2-3 million times that of the equivalent of oil and coal. Yet, we still focus our efforts on maintaining using the highly inefficient fossil fuels
in an effort to maintain the profitable status quo of oil and coal barons. 

The Landian technofeudalists in Silicon Valley trying to achieve 'generative AI' are the biggest example of everything that is wrong in society
being the mainstream goal of the select few in power. With connections to the military industrial complex, the growing police state, and financiers
of capital, they trade our data and are attempting to ensure that we the free are subjugated to their will; and they do everything possible to remind
us that we are powerless. They have added their own sinister twist to the story of Prometheus, and are using their creation to control humanity in
order to build their sick and twisted vision of the world. 

It was only natural of them to take our most advanced creation, and turn it into a machination for war, death, and money.
