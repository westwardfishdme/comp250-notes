# How To Get And Find Information For Our Research Papers

**Date:** 10-13-2026
**Keywords:**
Research Paper, Sourcing, Synthesizing Information

Today's lecture will be about getting information for our papers.

# Great places to start
Below are great ways to find good resources.

## Wikipedia 
Crazy intro, but this is actually very useful.

Wikipedia is great at getting information fast but not super
high-quality information. We can also use this as a starting
point for finding information for our papers.

## Library
The actual library can be a great place to collaborate with others,
along with finding good books.

## ACM digital library
ACM gives an abstract, document and several articles related to 
computing topics.
