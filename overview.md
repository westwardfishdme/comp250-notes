## Week 0 
Week 0 was an introduction to the course. Granted we overviewed some course related stuff-- what
we are going to cover in the class-- how we plan about doing so... but things got interesting very fast.

Off the bat, we already began discussing the controversies that surround AI-- how they impact writing, and
the world. I am very strict on my own personal AI usage, but the course is opening my eyes to see why people
use it for generating text. Most of my research during the course so far has been the impacts and 
implications that AI has on society as a whole. What this looks like in the current day is nothing short
from horrifying when you analyze it from a perspective of who owns the most valuable of current commodities:
**Cloud Capital**.

## Week 1
Week 1 was mostly about tooling and about how to effectively write notes. As documented in 
[zettelkasten.md](./week1/01-zettelkasten.md), it has inspired me to develop some tooling
that might prove useful to me and the course should I spend some time building it. 

On top of that, there is a lot of tooling that was introduced on how to be better organized
in regards to research and writing using tools such as zotero, git, and pandoc.

Although I myself have familiarity with some of the tools introduced, and using some alternatives
that are not listed as a part of the course; A lot of the tooling introduced has already been proving
themselves useful in courses outside of this class and I am extremely glad to have been introduced to them.
The primary tools that come to mind are `pandoc` and `zotero`, both of which made my life easier when it
comes to writing reports or writeups in several different formats that require a ton of time to put together.

`pandoc` in particular is a tool that I have been looking for since forever. Being able to write something in
markdown, or some other text format and being able to convert it to `.docx` or a `.pdf` is a game changer when
it comes to converting files used by professors who prefer non-free file formats for their coursework. 
Seriously, I can't express enough how much of a godsend `pandoc` really is for me.
