## Effective Writing
Research writing should be like story-telling. 
- Abstracts should be short and simple.
- You have to explain why the research matters; what problem you are addressing and it's importance
> "What matters is what's happening in the real world around us."

> "Writing is not just putting words to paper"

A lot of papers are driven by 'hope'
